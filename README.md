# Night Lamp
Hope lives in the form of light—and it’s yours to give. You’ll need to spend your light, your time, and your patience to brighten the worlds of those lost in despair. But every act of giving is a sacrifice: too little, and hope slips away; too much, and it fades into ignorance.

Even when you succeed, the satisfaction is fleeting—the journey repeats, and new voices call out in the dark. In Night Lamp, sacrifices must be made.
